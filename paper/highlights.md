# Highlights

- Connector wear on the OBD-II port is detected from the attached device's own telemetry, with no added hardware.
- Excitation coherence (losses rise with the car's own engine and road excitation) separates wear from look-alike faults.
- On six unseen vehicles, a full-bus tool detects wear with AUROC 0.880 and confirms moderate wear within two minutes of driving.
- A first-principles observability law predicts which wear stages each tool type can detect (Spearman 0.93).
- Evaluated on five public real-vehicle datasets, including a 381-vehicle fleet, with all code released.
